VERTICAL-VIDEO ADVERSARIAL-MASK REVIEW WORKSHEET
Version 1.0 | Original method kit by Alfred

PURPOSE
Use conservative, explicit masks to reveal when essential vertical-video content depends on uncertain edge space. This worksheet records a local design test. It does not reproduce a destination interface or guarantee that any platform, player, device, locale, caption renderer, or account state will behave like the masks.

STATE BOUNDARY
This record may support only: “The exact candidate was checked against the named masks with the recorded sample map and review passes.” It does not prove upload, destination processing, caption preservation, rights or policy clearance, accessibility conformance, public visibility, audience response, customer activity, or revenue.

Use synthetic fixtures only in the harness test. Do not paste private account screens, customer material, credentials, personal information, or copied platform imagery into this record.
