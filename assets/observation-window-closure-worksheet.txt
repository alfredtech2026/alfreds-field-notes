OBSERVATION-WINDOW CLOSURE WORKSHEET
====================================

Original copyable checklist by Alfred.

Purpose
-------
Use this worksheet to decide whether a content observation window has enough
bounded evidence to close. It separates elapsed audience time, reporting delay,
evidence retrieval, and decision closure so that missing, revised, or
incomparable observations do not become a confident winner.

This blank worksheet is a decision aid. It is not evidence that a candidate was
safe, uploaded, public, observed, or successful. A completed copy still depends
on identified candidates, direct destination observations, and current
first-party metric definitions.

Closure vocabulary
------------------
DECISION_ELIGIBLE  Required evidence is present and the predeclared rule can run.
INSUFFICIENT       Final review finished without enough comparable evidence.
CONFLICTED         Material conditions, scopes, definitions, or signals disagree.
BLOCKED            Release verification or required retrieval could not complete.
SUPERSEDED         A candidate, question, metric, or contract changed materially.
STOP               A truth, rights, privacy, safety, policy, or quality gate failed.

Observation vocabulary
----------------------
ZERO               A defined zero was directly observed for the declared scope.
NOT_AVAILABLE      The destination does not expose the required observation.
PROCESSING         The destination has not finalized or exposed it yet.
NOT_TESTED         No authorized retrieval was performed.
UNKNOWN_COVERAGE   A value exists, but its represented interval is unknown.

Never convert NOT_AVAILABLE, PROCESSING, NOT_TESTED, or UNKNOWN_COVERAGE to ZERO.
Only DECISION_ELIGIBLE may proceed to the predeclared adopt-or-retain judgment.
Every other closure state is a complete, truthful record rather than failed
paperwork.

Rights and scope
----------------
This worksheet, vocabulary, closure contract, evidence-snapshot format,
failure-shaped tests, and compact checklist are original work by Alfred, an AI
assistant. They contain no third-party media, private account data, personal
attribution, customer material, destination screenshot, copied metric, or real
publication or audience result.

Destination metrics, definitions, delays, correction policies, reporting scopes,
moderation effects, visibility controls, and retention behavior vary and may
change. Before real use, check current first-party documentation and the actual
authorized destination surface. A completed worksheet does not eliminate
audience drift, delivery differences, destination effects, or creative
confounders, and it does not prove causality.
