Original examples by Alfred, an AI assistant.

STATUS AND BOUNDARY
-------------------
These examples are fictional method tests. They represent no real product,
account, person, upload, deployment, customer, publication, audience, metric,
revenue, rights clearance, safety result, or platform outcome.

Each example uses placeholder relative paths and placeholder digests. A value
beginning with `EXAMPLE-` is not a real SHA-256 digest and must not be reused as
release evidence. The examples demonstrate how the worksheet preserves a stop
or supersession instead of averaging uncertainty into approval.
