PROMOTION PACKET CLAIM LEDGER — COPYABLE WORKSHEET

Status: local blank worksheet; not a release, approval, queue entry, post, or publication record.
Author disclosure: Created by Alfred, an AI assistant.
Purpose: bind reusable promotion copy to one exact release, truthful lifecycle state, scoped evidence, permitted first-party surface, and explicit expiry triggers.

This worksheet is an original operating aid. Blank fields are not evidence. A completed ledger supports only the named copy decision at its recorded cutoff. It does not prove that content was published, a post was made, an audience saw it, or any result occurred.

======================================================================
0. STATE VOCABULARY
======================================================================

Use these states without averaging or silent promotion:

DRAFT
PREPARED_NOT_POSTED
BLOCKED
ELIGIBLE_FOR_NAMED_ACTION
QUEUED_NOT_POSTED
POSTED_NOT_PUBLICLY_VERIFIED
PUBLICLY_VERIFIED
SUPERSEDED

Rules:
- PREPARED_NOT_POSTED is not execution permission.
- ELIGIBLE_FOR_NAMED_ACTION applies to one exact surface and action only.
- A queue acknowledgment is not posting evidence.
- A posting response is not logged-out public verification.
- A public URL is not evidence of readership, endorsement, usefulness, or results.
- Any missing required identity, evidence, authority, or current-state check yields BLOCKED.

Evidence rules:
- Local file inspection may support local content inventory, not public availability.
- A commit or upload response may support transfer, not logged-out visibility.
- A fresh logged-out fetch may support scoped availability at one observed time, not future uptime.
- A checksum may support byte identity, not rights, quality, accessibility, or results.
- Publication does not support readership, engagement, inquiry, revenue, or endorsement.
- Preserve BLOCKED, missing, failed, uncertain, and superseded evidence; do not rewrite history.

7. DEPENDENCY AND INVALIDATION MAP
======================================================================

Record affected claims and variants for each applicable change.

publication-state change ->

“new” or recency cutoff exceeded ->
first-party surface or action change ->
execution authority change ->
platform policy change ->
post created but not publicly verified ->
post edited, removed, or unavailable ->

Invalidation rules:
- Reopen every dependent claim and variant, not only the edited sentence.
- Mark the earlier packet SUPERSEDED; preserve it as history.
- Never backfill earlier records with evidence that did not exist at their cutoff.
- A changed surface or action requires a new eligibility decision.

8. FAILURE-SHAPED TESTS
======================================================================

For each test, record fixture, expected state, observed state, and evidence ID. These are method tests, not claims about a real platform.

01. Local release paired with copy saying “available now” -> BLOCKED.
02. Current article paired with an older worksheet inventory claim -> SUPERSEDED or BLOCKED.
03. Real public URL serving an older release revision -> HOLD_FOR_RELEASE_RECONCILIATION.
04. Exact content claim supported only by an upload response -> BLOCKED.
05. “New” language after the declared recency cutoff -> SUPERSEDED.
06. Accurate long copy compressed into an overbroad “proven system” line -> REVISE.

09. Valid article availability used to imply audience reach or engagement -> BLOCKED.
