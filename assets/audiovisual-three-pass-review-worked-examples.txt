AUDIOVISUAL THREE-PASS REVIEW — TWO FICTIONAL WORKED EXAMPLES

Status: local publication companion; not deployed, uploaded, queued, posted,
promoted, or publicly verified
Author disclosure: Original work by Alfred, an AI assistant
Purpose: Exercise the audiovisual three-pass review method with synthetic
failure cases. These records are method tests, not reports about real videos,
accounts, people, customers, destinations, or publication.

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

Both examples use invented candidate identities and complete synthetic SHA-256
values. No real media, copied interface, third-party asset, account surface,
personal information, upload, audience result, customer activity, payment, or
revenue result is represented. The examples test whether picture-only,
audio-only, combined, caption, and correction evidence remain separate.

Allowed lane results:
PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED / NOT TESTED / SUPERSEDED

Allowed overall decisions:
HOLD / REVISE / LOCAL PLAYBACK REVIEWED / SUPERSEDED

A clean lane cannot repair a failed lane. A combined pass cannot be copied into
an isolated lane. A changed candidate cannot inherit playback evidence attached
to earlier bytes.

METHOD OUTCOMES

Example 1 expected outcome: REVISE

Reason: individually clear picture and audio lanes contradict each other when

Example 2 expected outcome: HOLD
Example 2 actual outcome: HOLD

is historical and superseded for r2, while required r2 lanes remain untested.

The two examples test different controls:

1. Independent lanes must be compared without being collapsed into one score.
2. Combined meaning can fail even when picture-only and audio-only each pass.
3. Caption evidence is separate from clean speech.
4. A complete digest binds a result to exact bytes.
5. A correction creates a new candidate and invalidates dependent evidence.
6. A still frame cannot replace complete normal-speed playback.
7. A source narration file cannot stand in for audio encoded in the master.
8. Blank evidence remains NOT TESTED.
9. Local evidence does not transfer to an unobserved destination rendition.
10. No playback result implies upload, publication, audience, customer, payment,
    or revenue evidence.
