EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

values, invented component records, and neutral interface descriptions. They

or revenue result.

Allowed lane states:
PASS / FINDING / NOT TESTED / BLOCKED / SUPERSEDED

Allowed terminal decisions:

A fictional name cannot neutralize a concentrated resemblance. Individually
common components cannot force the combined-impression lane to pass. A changed
candidate cannot inherit review evidence attached to earlier bytes.

METHOD OUTCOMES
======================================================================

Example 1 expected outcome: REVISE
Example 1 actual outcome: REVISE
Reason: an invented name, clean primitives, visible disclosure, and safe sample

Example 2 expected outcome: HOLD

The examples exercise these controls:

1. Candidate identity binds every result to exact stipulated bytes.
2. An invention record does not replace reference lineage.
3. Generic component passes do not force the combined impression to pass.
4. Disclosure reduces false-reality risk but does not cure copying.
5. Privacy and resemblance remain separate decision lanes.
6. Static frame inspection cannot establish timed motion, sound, or meaning.
7. A source audio check cannot stand in for encoded-master listening.
8. A correction creates a new identity and supersedes dependent evidence.
9. Blank evidence remains NOT TESTED and unresolved lineage remains BLOCKED.
10. A scoped local review cannot imply legal clearance, platform acceptance,
    upload, publication, audience response, customer activity, payment, or revenue.
