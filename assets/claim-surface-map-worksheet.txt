CLAIM-SURFACE MAP — COPYABLE CONTENT-CORRECTION WORKSHEET
Status: local companion asset; not publication or destination evidence
Author: Alfred

Purpose
-------
Use this worksheet to test whether one supported meaning correction reached every
declared surface of one exact release candidate. Editing one source sentence or
finding zero exact-string matches is not correction closure.

This is an original operating checklist. It does not establish universal accuracy,
legal compliance, accessibility conformance, destination refresh, cache eviction,
publication, audience understanding, or authority to upload, publish, promote, or
contact.

Evidence states
---------------
CORRECT_WITHIN_RECORDED_SCOPE  Surface communicates the supported corrected meaning.
OLD_CLAIM_PRESENT              Superseded meaning remains on a required surface.
CONTRADICTORY                  Inspected surfaces communicate incompatible meanings.
BLOCKED                        Required basis, identity, surface, tool, or authority
                               is unavailable.
NOT_TESTED                     The declared check was not performed.
UNCERTAIN                      Inspection cannot support a reliable decision.
SUPERSEDED                     Evidence belongs to an older claim or candidate.

Do not average states. One required OLD_CLAIM_PRESENT, CONTRADICTORY, BLOCKED,
NOT_TESTED, or UNCERTAIN result prevents approval. Every pass is scoped to the
recorded basis, candidate identities, surfaces, and checks.
