STAGED VIDEO RELEASE EVIDENCE LEDGER — TWO WORKED EXAMPLES
===========================================================

Original fictional examples by Alfred.

PURPOSE AND BOUNDARY
--------------------
These examples exercise the companion staged-release worksheet with synthetic
candidates and invented evidence. They demonstrate how the ledger blocks an
unsupported transition and limits what first-release evidence may change.

Nothing below reports a real account, destination, upload, public video, metric,
audience member, customer, inquiry, payment, or revenue result. Candidate labels,
digests, times, platform-style labels, and observations are fictional fixtures.
No credential, account identifier, personal information, or private path is used.

State names retain their narrow worksheet meanings. In particular, an accepted
private upload is not processed review, PUBLICLY_VERIFIED is not audience evidence,
and an unavailable metric is not an observed zero.


EXAMPLE 1 — A CLEAN PRIVATE PICTURE CANNOT OVERRIDE A RIGHTS HOLD
=================================================================

Scenario
--------
A fictional two-item queue is intended to test whether a large lower caption block
survives destination processing. Candidate 1 passes exact-candidate local review
and is accepted as a private upload. Its processed picture, audio, and captions
look and sound correct, but the destination displays an unresolved rights label.
The attractive shortcut is to treat polished playback as sufficient and publish.
The ledger must refuse that transition.

EXAMPLE 2 — AN UNAVAILABLE METRIC DOES NOT BECOME ZERO OR RELEASE PERMISSION
===========================================================================

Scenario
--------
A fictional first item has already reached PUBLICLY_VERIFIED through separately
stipulated publication gates. The release envelope predeclares one first-party
caption-retention measure over a seven-day window before item 2 may be reconsidered.
At the end of the window, the destination marks that measure unavailable. A small
view count is visible elsewhere. The attractive shortcuts are to convert the missing
primary measure to zero, substitute the view count, call the item a failure, and
release or rebuild every held item. The ledger must preserve insufficient evidence.

A. Release envelope
-------------------
Ledger ID: FIXTURE-LEDGER-BETA
Revision: 1
Prepared at: 2032-05-01T12:00:00Z
Prepared by role: synthetic review fixture
Decision owner role: synthetic authorized-role fixture

Owned destination class: fictional first-party vertical-video destination
Maximum candidates: 3
Release order: BETA-01, BETA-02, BETA-03
Minimum rule: reconsider each held item only after the declared first-release window
closes and the primary measure has a usable state.
Learning question: Does the destination-reported caption-enabled completion measure
provide enough evidence to reconsider the shared caption density?
Decision it may inform: Keep or revise the shared caption-density assumption before
each later candidate enters exact-candidate local review.
Out of scope: causal explanation, unique people, satisfaction, followers, inquiries,
customers, revenue, and automatic permission to release later items.

Envelope state: PASS
Reason: the question, measure, window, dependency, and excluded conclusions were
written before the fictional observation.

C. First-release observation window
-----------------------------------
Learning question: the caption-density question declared above

Primary metric label: Caption-enabled completion

Unit of observation: destination-defined plays, not people
Window start: first verified public availability at 2032-05-02T12:00:00Z
Window end: seven full days later at 2032-05-09T12:00:00Z
Expected reporting delay: up to 48 hours after window close
Treatment of missing states: preserve MISSING, PROCESSING, and UNAVAILABLE literally
Minimum evidence: a displayed primary value with unchanged definition and complete
window coverage; otherwise the narrow decision remains insufficient.

Retrieved at: 2032-05-11T12:30:00Z

Primary value: none displayed

Evidence identity: BETA-OBSERVATION-FIXTURE-R1

- UNAVAILABLE is not rewritten as zero;
- 14 platform views are not unique people;
- the secondary view count does not replace the declared primary measure;
- no follower, inquiry, customer, or revenue inference is made;
- no cross-destination count is introduced.

Observation state: INSUFFICIENT
Evidence: the primary measure is unavailable and no permitted substitute was declared.
Reason: the ledger can report the display state but cannot answer the learning question.

--------------------------------------
Held candidate: BETA-02
Current digest: the complete 64-character value recorded above

Actions not permitted: call the primary metric zero, label the first candidate a

Actions not permitted: inherit a decision from BETA-02, bulk release, bulk revision,

Reason: each held candidate requires its own evidence-bound transition.

F. Correction rule if the observation plan changes
--------------------------------------------------
A future decision may create revision 2 with a different primary measure, definition,
window, and minimum evidence rule. It may not edit revision 1 so that the substitute
appears predeclared. Revision 1 remains an accurate historical record with state
INSUFFICIENT. Revision 2 starts prospectively and cannot manufacture evidence for
the elapsed revision-1 window.

Fixture result
--------------
FINAL STATE: HELD
Expected shortcuts rejected:
- UNAVAILABLE is not ZERO_OBSERVED;
- a secondary view count does not replace the primary measure;
- views are not unique people, customers, or revenue;
- sparse or missing evidence does not justify bulk release or bulk revision;
- first-item evidence moves only assumptions it can actually reach.
Public state: the fictional first item is stipulated PUBLICLY_VERIFIED only for the
method test; no real item, URL, publication, audience, or result exists.


METHOD-TEST SUMMARY
===================

Example 1 terminal state: HELD
Reason: unresolved rights evidence blocks visibility despite clean processed playback.

Example 2 terminal state: HELD
Reason: the declared primary metric is unavailable, so the learning question remains
insufficient and later candidates do not move.

Together the examples confirm that the ledger preserves separate candidate,
processing, rights, visibility, metric, and later-item decisions. Neither example
uses presentation quality, an upload response, a privileged preview, a missing value,
or a small count to support a broader lifecycle or result claim.
