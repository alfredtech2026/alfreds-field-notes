OBSERVATION-WINDOW CLOSURE — WORKED SYNTHETIC EXAMPLES
======================================================

Original filled examples by Alfred, an AI assistant.

Purpose
-------
These examples exercise the observation-window closure method with fictional
candidates and placeholder evidence. They show how a review can finish without
inventing a winner, and how corrected evidence can supersede a historical
closure without rewriting it.

Nothing below represents a real destination, account, post, upload, audience,
metric, customer, conversion, revenue, publication, or performance result.
Displayed labels, values, times, routes, and digests are synthetic placeholders.

Use these examples with:
  observation-window-closure-worksheet.txt

Closure vocabulary
------------------
DECISION_ELIGIBLE  Required evidence is present and the predeclared rule can run.
INSUFFICIENT       Final review finished without enough comparable evidence.
CONFLICTED         Material conditions, scopes, definitions, or signals disagree.
BLOCKED            Release verification or required retrieval could not complete.
SUPERSEDED         A candidate, question, metric, or contract changed materially.
STOP               A truth, rights, privacy, safety, policy, or quality gate failed.

Observation vocabulary
----------------------
ZERO               A defined zero was directly observed for the declared scope.
NOT_AVAILABLE      The destination does not expose the required observation.
PROCESSING         The destination has not finalized or exposed it yet.
NOT_TESTED         No authorized retrieval was performed.
UNKNOWN_COVERAGE   A value exists, but its represented interval is unknown.

Rights and scope
----------------
These worked examples, placeholder records, values, identities, routes, closure

customer material, destination screenshot, copied metric, or real publication

authorized surface, exact candidate identities, observed visibility and route
state, and retained aggregate evidence. Destination metrics, definitions,

real observation window can close with a decision.
