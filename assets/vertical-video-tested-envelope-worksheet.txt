LIFECYCLE STATE
ARTIFACT ROLE: ORIGINAL PUBLIC TEMPLATE; COMPLETION IS NOT RELEASE APPROVAL

PURPOSE
Replace a generic safe-zone rectangle with a versioned review of the exact
candidate, scenes, elements, occluders, viewer states, and acceptance rules.
This worksheet records bounded evidence. It does not prove universal
legibility, destination behavior, upload, publication, reach, or performance.

USE BOUNDARY
- Use original, licensed, consented, or explicitly synthetic material only.
- Do not enter personal data, account details, credentials, private paths,
  customer material, or unredacted destination screenshots.
- Stop when a test requires a password, CAPTCHA, identity check, payment,
  legal acceptance, or other user-presence gate.
- Label local simulation separately from observed destination evidence.
- Keep NOT_TESTED and UNCERTAIN states open; never convert them to PASS.
- Bind every result to one immutable candidate revision or byte identity.

EVIDENCE VOCABULARY
PASS          Required check completed within its declared scope.
FAIL          Required check completed and acceptance criteria were not met.
BLOCKED       Required check could not proceed because a prerequisite failed.
NOT_TESTED    No eligible evidence was collected for the declared state.
SUPERSEDED    Historical evidence exists but a relevant change invalidated it.
UNCERTAIN     Evidence exists but cannot support a reliable scoped decision.

CLEAR          No prohibited overlap; declared buffer remains.
TOUCHING_LIMIT No overlap, but a required buffer is consumed.
OCCLUDED       A prohibited intersection exists.
NOT_MEASURED   Geometry was not established.
STALE          Element, scene, occluder, viewport, or candidate changed.
