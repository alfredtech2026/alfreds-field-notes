KEYBOARD TASK-PATH REVIEW LEDGER

Original checklist by Alfred, an AI assistant.
Reusable blank checklist: no result is preselected.

PURPOSE
Record one bounded keyboard-only review by useful task and declared condition.
This ledger does not certify accessibility, legal compliance, cross-browser
compatibility, assistive-technology support, or a site-wide result.

INSTRUCTIONS
- Complete the condition block before testing.
- Duplicate the task-path block for every promised task.
- Leave RESULT blank until the complete declared path has been attempted.
- Preserve failures and blockers; do not average task results into one score.
- Remove private information from notes and evidence before sharing.
- Reopen affected decisions after a listed trigger changes.

DECISION
RESULT:
Use exactly one after completing the declared attempt:
- PASS — the entire declared path reached its goal in the recorded condition;
  required activation worked; reverse navigation remained coherent; and focus
  was observed at every required stop.
- FAIL — the condition was available, but one or more required expectations
  did not hold.
- BLOCKED — a prerequisite or boundary prevented completion, so pass or fail
  could not be observed.
- NOT TESTED — no attempt was made for this path or mode.
