PRIVATE-UPLOAD REVIEW — TWO WORKED SYNTHETIC EXAMPLES
====================================================

Original worked examples by Alfred, an AI assistant.

Purpose
-------
These examples show how to use the private-upload review worksheet when a
release-critical lane fails or a reviewed surface changes. Both traces are
fictional. They represent no real destination, account, upload, media item,
person, customer, audience, metric, publication, or business result.

The placeholder identities and observations below are teaching data. They are
not evidence that a transfer occurred, that a destination was private, that a
candidate was approved, or that anything became public.

Evidence vocabulary
-------------------
PASS         Identified evidence satisfies the declared check.
FAIL         Identified evidence contradicts the declared check.
BLOCKED      The required surface cannot be inspected.
NOT_TESTED   No result was collected.
SUPERSEDED   The result belongs to an older artifact, setting, or rendition.
UNCERTAIN    Observations exist but do not support a safe decision.

Safe rule: one release-critical FAIL, BLOCKED, NOT_TESTED, SUPERSEDED, or
UNCERTAIN lane prevents a proceed decision. Passing lanes are not averaged into
a score.

COMPACT LESSONS FROM BOTH EXAMPLES
==================================

1. A completed transfer is not processed-surface approval.
2. Picture and audio passes cannot cancel a caption failure.
3. Review the destination-attached caption track, not only a local caption file.
4. Requested privacy and an account label are not universal privacy evidence.
5. Keep untested retrieval paths visible and bounded by the declared contract.
6. Bind decisions to exact master, destination, rendition, caption, preview,
   metadata, visibility, and rights-policy identities.
7. Use HOLD_FOR_REPAIR when one release-critical surface fails.
8. Use SUPERSEDE_AND_REVIEW_AGAIN after an effect-shaping surface changes.
9. Preserve historically valid evidence without applying it to a replacement.
10. PROCEED_TO_PUBLICATION_GATE authorizes only entry to a separate gate.
11. Keep visibility transition and logged-out verification distinct.
12. Do not infer public availability, audience, performance, conversion, or
    continued availability from private-review evidence.
