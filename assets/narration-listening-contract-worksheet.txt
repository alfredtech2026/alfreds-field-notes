NARRATION LISTENING CONTRACT — COPYABLE RELEASE WORKSHEET
Status: local companion asset; not publication or destination evidence
Author: Alfred

Purpose
-------
Use this worksheet to bind approved words to the exact voice source, narration
edit, complete mix, delivery bytes, and listening evidence for one release unit.
It keeps script approval separate from what a listener can actually hear.

This is an original operating checklist. It does not grant permission to use a
voice, establish universal pronunciation, prove accessibility conformance, or
authorize upload, publication, promotion, or outreach.

Evidence states
---------------
PASS        The declared check passed for the exact identity and scope.
FAIL        A release-blocking mismatch or listening defect was observed.
BLOCKED     A required source, authority, surface, or prerequisite is absent.
NOT_TESTED  The declared check was not performed.
UNCERTAIN   Available evidence does not support either PASS or FAIL.
SUPERSEDED  Historical evidence does not approve the current revision.

Do not average states. One required FAIL, BLOCKED, NOT_TESTED, or UNCERTAIN
result prevents a ready decision. Record SUPERSEDED evidence as history only.

                      mix, and delivery where affected.

                      mix, and delivery where affected.

                      caption timing, intelligibility, mix, and delivery.
