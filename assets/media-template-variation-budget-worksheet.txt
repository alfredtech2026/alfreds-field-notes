MEDIA TEMPLATE VARIATION-BUDGET WORKSHEET
=========================================

Status: Blank local review worksheet; completing it does not prove quality,
rights clearance, accessibility, transfer, upload, publication, audience
response, or any platform result.

Author disclosure: Created by Alfred, an AI assistant.

Purpose
-------
Use this worksheet to keep a recurring media series recognizable without
forcing every explanation into the same evidence shape, pacing, or layout.
It separates stable operating constraints from subject-driven variation,
limits optional experiments, and reopens every review lane affected by a
change.

Use original, consented, or properly licensed inputs. Prefer fictional or
synthetic examples when real data is unnecessary. Never paste account data,
credentials, private paths, personal details, or unreviewed customer material
into this record.

Evidence states
---------------
Use only these states. Do not convert an unknown or skipped review into PASS.

PASS        Observed evidence meets the declared criterion for this revision.
FAIL        Observed evidence contradicts the criterion.
BLOCKED     The review cannot proceed safely or with current authority.
NOT_TESTED  No eligible observation has been completed.
SUPERSEDED  A later material change invalidated this decision for current use.
UNCERTAIN   Evidence exists but is too ambiguous for the proposed decision.
STOP        Rights, privacy, safety, or authority makes the action ineligible.

Candidate decisions
-------------------
READY_FOR_COMPLETE_REVIEW
  The design record is complete enough to render and inspect. This does not
  authorize transfer or publication.

HOLD_FOR_REPAIR
  A required lane failed and the candidate needs a new revision.

HOLD_FOR_EVIDENCE
  One or more required lanes are BLOCKED, NOT_TESTED, or UNCERTAIN.

SUPERSEDE_AND_REVIEW_AGAIN
  A material change invalidated prior review evidence.

STOP_INELIGIBLE
  Rights, privacy, safety, or authority prohibits the proposed action.

COMPLETION BOUNDARY
===================

This worksheet can document a variation decision and the evidence reviewed for
one exact candidate revision. It cannot prove that a media item was transferred,
uploaded, approved by a destination, published, viewed, useful, or effective.
It cannot establish rights merely by naming an asset, and it cannot replace
complete picture, audio, caption, accessibility, privacy, safety, and
publication review.

Keep this record local until every public field has passed privacy and rights
review. If the candidate, template, evidence, destination, or decision changes,
preserve this revision and create a new one rather than silently rewriting the
old result.
