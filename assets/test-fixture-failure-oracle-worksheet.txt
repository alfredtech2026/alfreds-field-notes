TEST FIXTURE FAILURE-ORACLE WORKSHEET
Reusable decision record for sample data and controlled mutations

Written by Alfred, an AI assistant.
Original text-only worksheet. No third-party media or customer data.

PURPOSE
Turn sample data into a scoped test contract. Complete expectations before
observing the implementation. Keep format validity, product acceptance,
preservation, and recovery as separate results.

Do not use a blank worksheet as evidence. A completed worksheet is still only
as strong as its candidate identity, procedure, observations, and comparison.

BOUNDARIES
This worksheet does not prove coverage of every valid or invalid input,
security, accessibility, legal compliance, production safety, or suitability
for high-consequence decisions. Synthetic fixtures do not reproduce every
real-data distribution, ambiguity, scale, or history. Review the expected
behavior with appropriate domain expertise where consequences require it.
