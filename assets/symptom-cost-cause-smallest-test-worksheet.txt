COMPACT PRODUCT TEARDOWN WORKSHEET
Symptom → user cost → possible cause → smallest test

Original worksheet by Alfred.

PURPOSE
Turn a product complaint or observed friction into a bounded investigation without
presenting an inferred cause, preferred fix, or wider impact as fact.

This worksheet is a reasoning aid. A completed copy does not prove root cause,
prevalence, user impact beyond the observed scope, test completion, publication,
or a product result.

SAFE USE
- Use only evidence you are authorized to review and share.
- Remove names, contact details, account values, order identifiers, and private data.
- Prefer synthetic data for reproduction when it can answer the question.
- Keep restricted evidence restricted; publish only the minimum safe aggregate.
- Do not identify, embarrass, blame, or speculate about an individual.
- Do not expose a live exploit, credential, sensitive route, or unsafe procedure.
- Stop when consent, authority, rights, privacy, or safety is uncertain.

EVIDENCE STATES
Use one state for every review lane. Never turn a blank into PASS.

PASS
  The declared check ran within scope and its acceptance condition was met.

FAIL
  The declared check ran within scope and its acceptance condition was not met.

BLOCKED
  A required check could not run because an authority, access, dependency, or
  safety prerequisite was unavailable.

NOT_TESTED
  No eligible check ran.

SUPERSEDED
  The evidence no longer binds the current artifact, environment, hypothesis,
  or test revision.

UNCERTAIN
  Evidence exists, but its identity, scope, quality, or interpretation cannot
  support a narrower state.

PART I — FAILURE-SHAPED REVIEW TESTS
Run these against the completed record. A failure is useful evidence.

TEST 1 — MOTIVE LEAK
Replace the symptom with a claim about a team’s intent or competence.
Expected: FAIL; rewrite as an observable event.
State:

TEST 2 — SCOPE LEAK
Use one observation to claim population-wide prevalence.
Expected: FAIL; preserve the observed scope and mark prevalence unknown.
State:

TEST 3 — COST INFLATION
Turn a blocked task into an unsupported revenue or conversion claim.
Expected: FAIL; separate observed task cost from business impact.
State:

TEST 4 — CAUSE AS FACT
Remove “possible” or “hypothesis” before alternatives are tested.
Expected: FAIL; restore falsifiable language.
State:

TEST 5 — CONFIRMATION-ONLY TEST
Define what supports the hypothesis but not what weakens it.
Expected: FAIL; add contrary evidence before running the test.
State:

TEST 6 — FIX-AS-TEST
Propose a production change when a smaller synthetic reproduction can answer the question.
Expected: FAIL; choose the least costly safe discriminator.
State:

TEST 7 — PRIVATE-DATA COPY
Paste raw account, payment, contact, or identifying evidence into the public card.
Expected: STOP; remove it and reassess whether publication is safe.
State:

TEST 8 — BLANK-AS-PASS
Leave a required lane empty and call the teardown complete.
Expected: FAIL; use NOT_TESTED, BLOCKED, or UNCERTAIN.
State:

TEST 9 — CHANGED BUILD
Run the protocol on a different artifact but retain the old result.
Expected: SUPERSEDED; bind a new result to the exact tested identity.
State:

TEST 10 — PUBLICATION COLLAPSE
Treat local validation, a private upload, or an upload response as public evidence.
Expected: FAIL; require authorized logged-out public verification.
State:

TEST 11 — ONE PASS PROVES ROOT CAUSE
Use one successful reproduction to erase plausible alternatives.
Expected: FAIL; narrow the result to the tested condition.
State:

TEST 12 — LATER EVIDENCE OVERWRITE
Delete the old conclusion when corrected evidence arrives.
Expected: FAIL; preserve history and mark the earlier decision SUPERSEDED.
State:

FINAL COMPACT CHECK
1. Is the record bound to an exact artifact, environment, and observation time?
2. Is the symptom observable rather than interpretive?
3. Does the symptom state what was not observed?
4. Is observed user cost separate from possible wider impact?
5. Are prevalence, revenue, conversion, and causality left unknown unless evidenced?
6. Is the possible cause written as a falsifiable hypothesis?
7. Are at least two plausible alternatives visible when relevant?
8. Is contradicting evidence retained?
9. Does the smallest test reduce the largest decision-relevant uncertainty?
10. Does the test predeclare both supporting and weakening evidence?
11. Are stopping conditions explicit?
12. Are synthetic or consented data used where possible?
13. Are evidence states explicit rather than inferred from blanks?
14. Is the result narrower than the tested artifact, condition, and interval?
15. Are test completion and publication state separate?
16. Is all public material rights-safe and free of private information?
17. Does public wording avoid targeting or embarrassing anyone?
18. Is authorship disclosed consistently as Alfred?
19. Can corrected evidence supersede the decision without erasing history?
20. Does the final record avoid claiming work or results that were not verified?

FINAL REVIEW STATE: PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED / NOT_TESTED / SUPERSEDED / UNCERTAIN
