REVIEW PACKET EVIDENCE INDEX — COPYABLE WORKSHEET
=================================================

Original worksheet by Alfred, an AI assistant.

PURPOSE
-------
Bind one review decision to one exact candidate, a closed inventory of required
evidence, scoped observations, explicit blind spots, and dependency-aware expiry
triggers. A completed index can support a narrow review decision; it does not by
itself prove rights, safety, quality, accessibility, transfer, publication,
audience response, or business results.

SAFE USE
--------
- Use synthetic examples or authorized material only.
- Record safe relative artifact names, never credentials, account data, contact
  details, notifications, hidden tabs, or private workstation paths.
- Capture the least revealing artifact that supports the declared observation.
- Keep restricted review material outside a public release bundle by default.
- Treat a redaction, crop, transcript, contact sheet, or converted file as a new
  artifact with its own lineage and limits.
- Preserve failures and superseded evidence as history; do not rewrite them into
  passes after repair.
- Keep local review, private transfer, destination processing, public release,
  and logged-out public verification as separate states.

EVIDENCE STATE VOCABULARY
-------------------------
PASS_WITHIN_DECLARED_SCOPE
  The item supports its named observation for the exact bound identity and
  declared method. It does not approve unrelated review lanes.

FAIL
  The declared check found a condition that blocks the requested decision.

BLOCKED
  A required identity, authority, input, tool, or dependency is unavailable.

MISSING
  A required artifact or observation is absent from the packet.

NOT_TESTED
  The required check was declared but not performed.

UNCERTAIN
  The artifact or method cannot support a reliable observation.

SUPERSEDED
  The evidence belongs to an older candidate, method, manifest, or decision
  basis. Preserve it, but do not use it for the current decision.

NOT_APPLICABLE_WITH_REASON
  The slot does not apply within the recorded scope, and the reason is explicit.

A required FAIL, BLOCKED, MISSING, NOT_TESTED, UNCERTAIN, or SUPERSEDED item is
non-passing. Do not average it against green rows.

Expected: BLOCKED; volume does not repair missing identity and scope.

Expected: SUPERSEDED; bind fresh evidence to the new byte identity.

Expected: FAIL; retain only the declared coarse picture observation.
State:

TEST 4 — CHECKSUM AS QUALITY REVIEW
Use a matching digest to claim the media is readable, rights-safe, and useful.
Expected: FAIL; byte equality cannot establish those lanes.
State:

TEST 5 — CLEAN PARSE AS CAPTION APPROVAL
Use syntactically valid timed text to claim timing and meaning are correct.
Expected: FAIL; perform separate timing and meaning review.
State:

TEST 6 — EMPTY REQUIRED ROW
Leave a required slot blank and close the packet because other rows pass.
Expected: MISSING or NOT_TESTED; the decision remains non-passing.

TEST 7 — HIDDEN FAILURE
Delete a failed row after repair and retain only the replacement pass.
Expected: FAIL; preserve history and supersede it explicitly.

TEST 8 — PRIVATE CAPTURE OVERCOLLECTION

Expected: FAIL; minimize or replace the artifact before retention.

TEST 9 — REDACTION WITHOUT LINEAGE

Expected: UNCERTAIN or FAIL; public evidence must support the public claim.

FINAL TWENTY-CHECK REVIEW
-------------------------
1. Is the requested decision explicit?
2. Are the exact candidate and manifest identified?
3. Are publication and destination states truthful?
4. Were required evidence slots declared before closure?
5. Do missing and failed slots remain visible?
6. Does every item have a stable evidence ID?
7. Does every item name its exact bound object?
8. Do generated items record method, input, parameters, output, and coverage?
9. Are artifacts, observations, and decisions recorded separately?
10. Does every observation name its scope and important blind spot?
11. Are rights, privacy, picture, sound, timed text, metadata, package, and
    destination lanes kept distinct?
12. Does every combined decision cite its required evidence IDs?
13. Is evidence from another candidate non-passing for the current candidate?
14. Are sensitive captures minimized and restricted artifacts excluded publicly?
15. Do BLOCKED, MISSING, NOT_TESTED, UNCERTAIN, and SUPERSEDED remain non-passing?
16. Does every pass have an invalidation trigger?
17. Do repairs add new evidence instead of rewriting failed history?
18. Is unaffected evidence reused only with an explicit independence basis?
19. Are local review and destination observation separate?
20. Does the final claim state both what was reviewed and what remains unknown?
