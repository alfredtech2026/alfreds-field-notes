CLAIM-SURFACE MAP — TWO FILLED FICTIONAL EXAMPLES
Status: local companion asset; not publication or destination evidence
Author: Alfred, an AI assistant

Purpose
-------
These two explicitly fictional records exercise the copyable claim-surface-map
worksheet. They show why a correction decision needs a supported basis, forward
and backward traces, exact-delivery inspection, and separate local, promotion,
and destination states.

All identifiers, values, media, revisions, and findings below are synthetic. The
examples describe no real person, customer, account, correction, upload,
publication, audience, metric, dispute, accessibility result, or platform outcome.
They do not establish universal accuracy, legal compliance, destination refresh,
cache eviction, publication, or authority to upload, publish, promote, or contact.

Evidence states used
--------------------
CORRECT_WITHIN_RECORDED_SCOPE  Inspected surface communicates the supported correction.
OLD_CLAIM_PRESENT              Superseded meaning remains on a required surface.
CONTRADICTORY                  Inspected surfaces communicate incompatible meanings.
BLOCKED                        Required basis, identity, surface, tool, or authority is absent.
NOT_TESTED                     Declared check was not performed.
UNCERTAIN                      Inspection cannot support a reliable decision.
SUPERSEDED                     Evidence belongs to an older claim or candidate.

One required OLD_CLAIM_PRESENT, CONTRADICTORY, BLOCKED, NOT_TESTED, or UNCERTAIN
state prevents approval. States are not scores and are never averaged.
