VERIFICATION EXPIRY WORKSHEET
A reusable decision record by Alfred

Purpose
-------
Use this worksheet to decide whether an earlier check still supports a named
action. It is a record template, not proof that any check ran or passed.

Keep the historical observation unchanged. If a later event breaks an
assumption, change the evidence state for the current decision rather than
rewriting the old result.

RESULT VOCABULARY
-----------------
PASS         The named check passed for its identified subject and scope.
FAIL         The named check found a defect in that subject and scope.
BLOCKED      The check could not be completed.
NOT TESTED   The lane was outside the performed scope.
SUPERSEDED   The observation remains historical evidence but may no longer
             authorize the current decision.

SYNTHETIC MINI-EXAMPLE
----------------------
This example represents no real release, person, customer, or destination.

Decision ID: demo-release-8
Decision to make: accept one article and one original vector card
Candidate revision: demo-8
Article digest: sha256:<article-digest>
Card digest: sha256:<card-digest>
Privacy observation: PASS for demo-8 text, metadata, filenames, and SVG text
Explicit exclusion: provider-generated preview was NOT TESTED
Invalidators: either digest changes; a file enters or leaves the bundle;
              metadata, privacy rules, destination, or provider output changes

Event: the card footer changes after the observation.
Historical result: preserve the original PASS for demo-8.
Current evidence state: SUPERSEDED for acceptance of the changed bundle.
Reason: the identified card digest no longer matches.
Named rechecks: privacy and rendering checks for the new card, followed by every
                release-wide consistency check required by the decision policy.
Decision state: HOLD until those checks produce eligible evidence.

BOUNDARIES
----------
A digest can identify bytes; it does not prove privacy, rights, quality,
accessibility, or destination behavior. A recent timestamp does not repair a
subject, scope, procedure, or destination mismatch. A provider success state
does not replace retrieval and inspection of the served result. Selective
rechecking is safe only when unchanged lanes and assumptions can be shown;
higher-consequence work may require a complete review after material change.
