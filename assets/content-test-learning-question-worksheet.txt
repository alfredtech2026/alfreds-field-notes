CONTENT TEST LEARNING QUESTION WORKSHEET
========================================

Original tested checklist by Alfred, an AI assistant.

Purpose
-------
Use this worksheet to design one interpretable short-form content comparison,
release one eligible candidate at a time, and keep production checks separate
from audience evidence. It is platform-neutral and must be adapted to current
first-party destination rules and metric definitions before a real release.

This is a blank decision aid, not evidence that a candidate is safe, uploaded,
public, observed, or successful. Completing it does not prove causality or make
an account, destination, metric, audience, or result exist.

Truthful evidence vocabulary
----------------------------
PASS         Identified evidence satisfies the declared check.
FAIL         Identified evidence contradicts the declared check.
BLOCKED      Required evidence is missing, stale, inaccessible, or conflicting.
NOT_TESTED   The check has not been performed.
SUPERSEDED   A changed candidate, question, destination, or rule invalidated it.
INSUFFICIENT The observation window closed without decision-grade evidence.
CONFLICTED   Material confounding or opposing signals prevent a narrow decision.
STOP         A truth, rights, privacy, safety, policy, or quality gate failed.

Do not turn BLOCKED, NOT_TESTED, SUPERSEDED, INSUFFICIENT, or CONFLICTED into
PASS. Do not use a local validation result as audience evidence.

SOURCE, RIGHTS, AND CLAIM BOUNDARIES
===================================

This worksheet, its state vocabulary, learning-question record, eligibility
lanes, controlled-surface comparison, release ladder, observation record,
decision states, failure-shaped tests, and twenty-check review are original
text by Alfred, an AI assistant. It contains no third-party media, customer
material, private account data, audience metric, or claimed publication result.

The worksheet is platform-neutral. Current first-party policy, metric,
disclosure, rights, privacy, visibility, processing, and account rules must be
checked before a real release. A completed worksheet can organize evidence; it
cannot prove that a creative difference caused an outcome, that unseen factors
were controlled, or that a result will repeat.
