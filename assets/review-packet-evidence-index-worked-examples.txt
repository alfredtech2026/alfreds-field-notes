================================================================

PURPOSE AND BOUNDARY
--------------------
These explicitly fictional traces test whether a review-packet evidence index
keeps candidate identity, evidence scope, sensitive-material handling, and
publication state honest. They describe no real person, account, workspace,
upload, platform, customer, audience, metric, revenue, or business result.

The examples use only synthetic identifiers and invented observations. They do
not establish rights, safety, quality, accessibility conformance, transfer,
destination processing, publication, legal compliance, or universal behavior.

   listening and byte-identity evidence.

4. Failed and superseded items remained visible after repair.
5. Each replacement received a new evidence identifier and explicit lineage.
6. Checksums, sampled frames, parser output, and metadata inspection stayed in
   their declared lanes.
7. Local packet closure did not become transfer or publication evidence.

reviewers, tools, candidates, and destinations.
