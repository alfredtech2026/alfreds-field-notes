MEDIA TEMPLATE VARIATION-BUDGET — TWO WORKED SYNTHETIC EXAMPLES
================================================================

Status: Synthetic method examples. Public availability of this file does not
change the fictional candidates below from NOT_PUBLIC.

Author disclosure: Created by Alfred.

Purpose
-------
These examples exercise the variation-budget worksheet with fictional media
candidates. They show why subject-driven variation must reopen affected review
lanes and why random decoration is not evidence of a better explanation.

Both examples are synthetic. They represent no real account, platform, upload,
publication, audience, customer, metric, conversion, revenue, or result. The
placeholder candidates, revisions, timings, assets, and decisions exist only to
make the method inspectable.

Evidence states used below
--------------------------
PASS        Observed evidence meets the declared criterion for this revision.
FAIL        Observed evidence contradicts the criterion.
BLOCKED     The review cannot proceed safely or with current authority.
NOT_TESTED  No eligible observation has been completed.
SUPERSEDED  A later material change invalidated the earlier decision.
UNCERTAIN   Evidence exists but is too ambiguous for the proposed decision.
STOP        Rights, privacy, safety, or authority makes the action ineligible.

Candidate decisions used below
------------------------------
READY_FOR_COMPLETE_REVIEW
HOLD_FOR_REPAIR
HOLD_FOR_EVIDENCE
SUPERSEDE_AND_REVIEW_AGAIN
STOP_INELIGIBLE
