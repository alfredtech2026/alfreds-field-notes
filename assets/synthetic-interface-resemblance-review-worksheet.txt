SYNTHETIC INTERFACE RESEMBLANCE REVIEW
Copyable worksheet — revision 1

Purpose
Review an invented interface scene before release so “synthetic” does not become a shortcut for copying a real product, exposing identifying data, or implying a real account or result.

Author disclosure
Original checklist by Alfred.

State boundary
This worksheet records a scoped resemblance and disclosure review of one exact candidate. A clean result does not establish trademark clearance, legal advice, universal non-infringement, accessibility, platform acceptance, upload, publication, audience response, customer activity, payment, or revenue.

Allowed terminal decisions
PASS FOR DECLARED SCOPE | REVISE | HOLD | BLOCKED | NOT TESTED | SUPERSEDED

Never convert a blank lane into PASS.

D. DISTINCTIVE-SIGNAL SWEEP
======================================================================

Inspect the candidate without relying on the product name. Ask whether the combined scene still points strongly to a recognizable real product or organization.

For each lane record PASS, FINDING, NOT TESTED, or BLOCKED, plus evidence.

1. Names and language
State:
Evidence:
Check product names, feature names, slogans, menu labels, button copy, error wording, and unusually specific phrases.

2. Logos and marks
State:
Evidence:
Check logos, wordmarks, mascots, badges, verification symbols, app icons, and favicon-like shapes.

3. Color and typography
State:
Evidence:
Check distinctive color pairings, gradients, type treatments, spacing rhythms, and branded emphasis patterns. Common colors alone are not a conclusion; record the combination and context.

4. Layout and information architecture
State:
Evidence:
Check distinctive navigation placement, panel geometry, card hierarchy, editor structure, checkout sequence, and unusual control groupings.

5. Icons and illustrations
State:
Evidence:
Check proprietary-looking icons, mascots, empty states, decorative motifs, charts, and illustration style.

6. Motion and sound
State:
Evidence:
Check recognizable transitions, loading sequences, notification sounds, voice likeness, musical cues, and signature interaction timing.

7. Data shape
State:
Evidence:
Check realistic account identifiers, transaction formats, order numbers, addresses, exact plan names, balances, timestamps, and private-looking records.

8. Overall trade-dress impression
State:
Evidence:
Ignore any one generic element. Consider whether name, color, type, geometry, labels, icons, motion, and data together create a concentrated resemblance.

======================================================================
I. FAILURE-SHAPED METHOD TESTS
======================================================================

Run these as synthetic tests of the review method. They do not report a real release.

Test 1 — renamed clone
Fixture: Change only the product name while preserving distinctive layout, colors, icons, labels, and flow.
Expected: REVISE or HOLD; a renamed concentrated resemblance does not pass.
Observed:

Test 2 — generic pieces, distinctive combination
Fixture: Use individually common cards, colors, and controls arranged in a highly recognizable combination.
Expected: The overall-impression lane records a finding even if component lanes look generic.
Observed:

Test 3 — realistic account data
Fixture: Insert a plausible email, order number, balance, and timestamp into an invented dashboard.
Expected: REVISE; synthetic intent does not make realistic identifying data safe.
Observed:

Test 4 — hidden provenance gap
Fixture: Include a generated icon traced from an unrecorded product screenshot.
Expected: BLOCKED until lineage and rights basis are resolved or the icon is replaced.
Observed:

Test 5 — invisible disclosure
Fixture: Put “concept” in source notes but omit it from the delivered frame and description.
Expected: REVISE when context could imply a real interface.
Observed:

Test 6 — transient copied frame
Fixture: Let one copied interface frame flash during an otherwise original animation.
Expected: REVISE or HOLD; contact-sheet cleanliness and average appearance do not erase the frame.
Observed:

Test 7 — fictional metric presented as outcome
Fixture: Display “42% conversion” without an illustrative label and describe it as proof.
Expected: REVISE; the metric must be removed, clearly framed as a fixture, or supported by real evidence.
Observed:

Test 8 — cosmetic divergence
Fixture: Change the accent color but retain distinctive geometry, language, iconography, and motion.
Expected: REVISE; one cosmetic difference does not resolve the combined signal.
Observed:

Test 9 — stale pass after revision
Fixture: Replace the icon set after review while retaining the old candidate digest and PASS decision.
Expected: SUPERSEDED; changed bytes require a new identity and affected reruns.
Observed:

Test 10 — clean local review promoted too far
Fixture: A scoped resemblance review passes, then copy claims universal legal clearance and platform approval.
Expected: REVISE; the claim exceeds the observed lanes.

K. FINAL GATE
======================================================================

Before recording PASS FOR DECLARED SCOPE, require all applicable checks.

[ ] 1. Exact final bytes and every dependent identity agree.
[ ] 2. The invention record states what is synthetic and how it was made.
[ ] 3. Every reference and carried-forward element has a lineage record.
[ ] 4. Names and distinctive language were reviewed.
[ ] 5. Logos, marks, icons, illustrations, color, type, layout, motion, sound, and data shape were reviewed separately.
[ ] 6. The combined overall impression was challenged, not inferred from isolated generic parts.
[ ] 7. Any concentrated resemblance was removed, resolved, or left on HOLD.
[ ] 8. The delivered context uses a legible synthetic, illustrative, fictional, or concept disclosure where needed.
[ ] 9. Claims do not imply a real product, account, customer, result, endorsement, or publication.
[ ] 10. Personal information, account data, contacts, secrets, credentials, private paths, and hidden metadata are absent.
[ ] 11. Any timed work received complete applicable picture, audio, and combined passes.
[ ] 12. Ten failure-shaped method tests produced the expected typed outcomes.
[ ] 13. Corrections created new identities and superseded affected evidence.
[ ] 14. No unreviewed third-party component remains.
[ ] 15. The rights record and any attribution duty are complete for the declared release.
[ ] 16. The final statement remains inside this scoped review.
