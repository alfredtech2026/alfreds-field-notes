AUDIOVISUAL THREE-PASS REVIEW WORKSHEET

Status: blank local review template
Author disclosure: Original work by Alfred
Purpose: Review one exact video candidate through picture-only, audio-only,
and combined playback without turning one ordinary playthrough into a broad
quality, rights, accessibility, platform, or publication claim.

USE BOUNDARY

This worksheet can record direct review of one identified local master. It does
not by itself prove caption conformance, accessibility conformance, rights or
policy clearance, deterministic reproduction, destination processing, public
availability, audience response, customer activity, or revenue. Use NOT TESTED
when a lane was not completed. Never convert missing evidence into PASS.

Allowed lane results:
PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED / NOT TESTED / SUPERSEDED

Allowed overall decisions:
HOLD / REVISE / LOCAL PLAYBACK REVIEWED / SUPERSEDED

A blank field is incomplete evidence, not a pass.

J. FAILURE-SHAPED METHOD TESTS
======================================================================

These tests exercise the worksheet, not a real release.

1. Review a file whose complete digest differs from the approved candidate.
   Expected: identity result FAIL or BLOCKED; playback approval is unavailable.
   Actual:

2. Complete one ordinary combined play and leave isolated passes blank.
   Expected: overall decision HOLD; blank passes do not inherit PASS.
   Actual:

3. Review a separate narration file instead of the master's audio.
   Expected: audio-only result BLOCKED.
   Actual:

4. Frame-step every cut but skip normal-speed playback.
   Expected: complete playback remains NOT TESTED.
   Actual:

5. Correct a visual title after all passes and retain the old approval.
   Expected: affected picture and combined evidence becomes SUPERSEDED.
   Actual:

6. Hear clean speech and mark captions PASS without reading them.
   Expected: caption result remains NOT TESTED.
   Actual:

7. Pass the local master and claim the destination transcode passed.
   Expected: destination rendition remains NOT TESTED.
   Actual:

8. Find identifying information in one transient frame.
   Expected: picture result FAIL and overall decision HOLD or REVISE.
   Actual:

9. Find unreviewed third-party audio under accurate narration.
   Expected: audio result FAIL; presentation quality cannot override rights.
   Actual:
