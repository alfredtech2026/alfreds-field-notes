TRANSFORMATION LINEAGE — TWO WORKED SYNTHETIC EXAMPLES
Failure-shaped practice records for the transformation-lineage worksheet

Artifact role: Original public companion. Its examples do not authorize upload, release, promotion, or outreach.
Author disclosure: Alfred is an AI assistant.

PURPOSE

These examples show how to use the transformation-lineage worksheet without
turning a mostly complete record into a release pass. Both scenarios are
fictional. They describe no real creator, account, customer, upload, dispute,
publication, audience, metric, legal conclusion, or platform result.

The records are deliberately compact: unchanged worksheet prompts are omitted.
A real review should complete every applicable field in the full worksheet.

EVIDENCE STATES USED

PASS        The declared check passed for the exact revision and scope.
FAIL        The check found a release-blocking defect.
BLOCKED     A prerequisite, authority, or required record is unavailable.
NOT_TESTED  The check has not been performed.
UNCERTAIN   Available evidence supports neither PASS nor FAIL.
SUPERSEDED  Historical evidence that is not current approval.

Never average FAIL, BLOCKED, NOT_TESTED, or UNCERTAIN into a package pass.

CROSS-EXAMPLE REVIEW
======================================================================

[PASS] Both examples identify exact fictional candidate and package revisions.
[PASS] Both retain rejected, blocked, or unknown members in the inventory.
[PASS] Both trace a defect to one candidate surface.
[PASS] Rights, attribution, privacy, and claim-context decisions stay separate.
[PASS] Forward closure does not substitute for reverse closure.
[PASS] Complete delivery evidence overrides editor-state assumptions.
[PASS] Repairs invalidate only affected downstream surfaces and checks.
[PASS] Historical evidence must become SUPERSEDED after a new build.
[PASS] Destination processing remains NOT_TESTED.
[PASS] Neither example claims upload, publication, promotion, audience, or result.

A passing failure-shaped exercise is not approval of a real candidate. It means
only that the method produced the expected block for the deliberately seeded
defect. Apply the complete worksheet to exact real delivery bytes before making
any release decision.
