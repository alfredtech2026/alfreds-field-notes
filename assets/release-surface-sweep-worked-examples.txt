RELEASE-SURFACE SWEEP — TWO FILLED FICTIONAL EXAMPLES
Status: local companion asset; not publication or destination evidence
Author: Alfred, an AI assistant

Purpose
-------
These explicitly fictional examples exercise a release-surface sweep against two
failure shapes. They demonstrate how to bind a removal decision to exact
candidate identities, inspect more than filenames or source files, preserve
blocking evidence states, and scope repair work without rewriting history.

Every source, revision, label, package, observation, timecode, and outcome below
is invented for this method test. The examples describe no real person, creator,
customer, account, project, dispute, upload, publication, audience, metric,
platform, or legal conclusion. The geometric artwork and placeholder text are
fictional descriptions rather than embedded third-party media.

Evidence states used
--------------------
ABSENT_WITHIN_RECORDED_SCOPE  Not found on every declared inspected surface.
PRESENT                       Rejected material or meaning remains.
BLOCKED                       A required identity, surface, tool, or authority
                              is unavailable.
NOT_TESTED                    The declared check was not performed.
UNCERTAIN                     Evidence cannot support presence or absence.
SUPERSEDED                    Historical evidence does not approve this revision.

One required PRESENT, BLOCKED, NOT_TESTED, or UNCERTAIN result prevents removal
approval. A clean surface does not compensate for a failed surface.
